Among the objectives of this dissertation is to leave, at its conclusion, a working 4G and 5G
network testbed in place at the university, rather than a single disposable experiment, so
that future projects can build on and explore it directly rather than starting from scratch.
This objective shapes several of the implementation choices described in
Chapter~\ref{cha:implementation}.
