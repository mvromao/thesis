\section{4G vs 5G}
\label{sec:4g-vs-5g}

This section presents a comparison of the 4G baseline against the 5G network across the three test rounds described in Section~\ref{sec:test-methodology}, focusing on the evolution of performance metrics. Because the 5G configuration changed between rounds, namely the TDD structure and the \texttt{rx\_gain} setting, these results are presented as a progression rather than a single snapshot, tracking how each change affected throughput at each location and in each direction. As will become clear over the following sections, no single round can be presented as \emph{the} definitive 5G result: the configuration that performs best at short range is not the same as the one that performs best at long range, a trade-off examined in more detail later in this chapter.
